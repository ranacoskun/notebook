## Definitions
- [[@malmbergCapturingTemporalSequential2017a]]: active, goal-directed process in which learners intentionally regulate their own cognition, metacognition, motivation and behavior; in collaboration, reflects each individual's contribution toward their own goals (p. 161)
- [[hadwin2017selfregulation]]: individual learners taking metacognitive control of cognitive, behavioral, motivational and emotional states through iterative planning, monitoring, evaluation and change (p. 1). In collaboration: deliberate, strategic metacognitive planning, task enactment, reflection and adaptation in a joint task (p. 6)

## Key components
- [[Task perceptions]]: learners' interpretation of the task (phase 1)
- [[Goal setting and planning]]: goals and plans based on task perceptions (phase 2)
- [[Task enactment]]: applying strategies to reach goals (phase 3)
- [[Adaptation]]: small scale (current task) or large scale (future tasks) change (phase 4)
- [[Metacognitive monitoring]]: comparing progress to standards; drives adaptation
- [[COPES]]: Conditions, Operations, Products, Evaluations, Standards
- Modes in collaboration: [[Co-regulated learning]], [[Socially shared regulation of learning]]

## Papers using this theory
- [[@malmbergCapturingTemporalSequential2017a]]: Winne & Hadwin phases as coding scheme for regulation processes; SRL/CoRL/SSRL as coding scheme for regulation types
- [[hadwin2017selfregulation]]: extends the Winne & Hadwin model to collaboration and defines three modes of regulation

## Notes
- Main model in both papers is Winne & Hadwin (1998); Zimmerman & Schunk (2011) cited as the macro-level alternative ([[hadwin2017selfregulation]], p. 4)
- Hadwin et al. stress that SRL is itself social, not the non-social counterpart of SSRL (p. 14)
