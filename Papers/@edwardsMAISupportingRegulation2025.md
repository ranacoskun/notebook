## Abstract
Collaborative learning requires groups of learners to engage in regulation of learning on individual, mutual, and group levels. Supporting regulation of learning in collaborative contexts is challenging however due to the multifaceted complexity of regulation and its nature as an agentive, self-guided process. This interactive demo presents MAI, a proactive AI agent which supports regulation of learning in collaborative groups by recognizing trigger contexts and prompting group members to raise their metacognitive awareness, enabling them to initiate regulatory processes at their own initiative.
